Simple rules for using SafeAlert — built by and for Nigerians.

Community-reported information

Map alerts come from community members, not official government sources. Information may be wrong or outdated.

Alerts need independent confirmations before verified. False reports may lead to account restrictions.

Use your own judgment before travelling or acting on an alert.

SOS & responding to others

🆘 Panic is not emergency services

SOS alerts your safety circle and opted-in helpers. We do not dispatch police, ambulance, or government.

🤝 Helper safety

Responding to a stranger carries real risk. Read our helper safety guide — go with someone, meet in public.

Acceptable use

You agree not to:

  • Submit false or malicious reports
  • Harass users or abuse the panic button
  • Trigger fake panics to lure or harm responders
  • Share someone's location without consent
  • Use the app to stalk or doxx others

We may suspend accounts or remove content. Repeated abuse leads to a permanent ban.

Age requirement

You must be at least 13. Under 18? Use with a parent or guardian.

Liability

Map data is provided without warranty. SafeAlert is not liable for harm from false reports, network failures, or decisions to travel or meet others.
