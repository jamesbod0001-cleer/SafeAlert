Terms of Use

Rules for using SafeAlert NG — a citizen safety network across Nigeria.

Citizen-reported data

Map alerts, route scores, and incident reports come from community members, not official government sources. Information may be incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate. Always use your own judgment and verify before acting.

Alerts need independent confirmations before they are marked verified. False reports harm your community and may lead to account restrictions.

Panic is not emergency services

SafeAlert does not dispatch police, ambulance, or any government agency. Panic alerts your chosen safety circle and opted-in nearby helpers. In a life-threatening emergency, call 112 or your local emergency number directly.

Abuse & moderation

You agree not to:

  • Submit false or malicious incident reports
  • Harass other users or abuse the panic feature
  • Impersonate others or share another person's location without consent
  • Attempt to disrupt or scrape the service

We may hide false reports, suspend accounts, or remove content that violates these rules. Repeated abuse results in permanent ban.

Minimum age

You must be at least 13 years old to use SafeAlert. If you are under 18, use the app with a parent or guardian's permission.

Contact

Report abuse or ask about these terms: support@safealert.ng
