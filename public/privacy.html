Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need. We don't sell your data or share it with government by default.

What we collect

📱 Phone & account

Your number is stored securely (hashed). Used only for sign-in and OTP.

📍 Location

Live GPS only during SOS or journey, or if you opt in to help nearby. Removed when you tap I'm Safe or end a journey.

👥 Safety circle

Contacts are encrypted. They get alerts only when you start a journey or SOS.

🗺️ Community reports

Reports you submit may stay on the map in anonymised form after account deletion.

Full breakdown: FAQ — Your data

What we never do

  • We do not sell phone numbers, locations, or contacts to advertisers.
  • We do not show strangers your exact home address.
  • We do not require national ID, BVN, or government ID.
  • We do not automatically send data to police or government.

Delete your account

You can remove your profile and personal data at any time.

1 Sign in to the app
2 Open Settings & account
3 Tap Delete my account and confirm

Can't access the app? Email privacy@safealert.ng with your phone number. We handle requests under Nigeria's NDPR.

Clearing app storage removes local caches (e.g. offline maps) on that device only.
