Privacy Policy

How SafeAlert handles your data — citizen network, not government surveillance.

Phone numbers

Your phone number is stored as a one-way SHA-256 hash, not in plain text. We use the hash only to verify OTP sign-in and match your account. We do not sell or share phone numbers with third parties.

Location data

Live GPS is shared only while a journey or panic is active, or when you opt in to help nearby. When your journey ends or you mark yourself safe after a panic, your live location is deleted from our servers.

Help nearby (opt-in)

Receiving alerts when someone near you needs help is off by default. You must turn on Help nearby in your profile. We never show your exact address in push notifications.

Safety circle

Names and phone numbers you add to your safety circle are encrypted at rest. Only you can view and manage your circle contacts. They receive alerts only when you trigger panic or start a journey.

No government data sharing

SafeAlert does not automatically share your data, location, or reports with police, military, or government agencies. Community alerts are citizen-reported. You choose who to call in an emergency.

Account deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by calling DELETE /user/account while signed in. This removes your profile, circle, panic history, and preferences.
