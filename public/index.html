Women's safety tips
Built for real journeys — not government dispatch
  • Add 3–5 trusted people to your circle before you travel
  • Schedule a check-in when alone at night or on long trips
  • Share your journey plan on WhatsApp — use Share journey above
  • Hold SOS if threatened — helpers see approximate area, not your exact door
  • Meet helpers in public, lit places — read helper safety FAQ
  • Call 112 for immediate danger — SafeAlert supplements, not replaces
Before you respond
Fake panics can be used to lure helpers. Stay safe.
  • Do not go alone — tell someone where you are going
  • Meet in a public, lit area if possible
  • Location shown is approximate (~hundreds of metres)
  • If it feels wrong, stop and call 112

Before you activate SOS
SafeAlert alerts your circle and nearby helpers. It does not dispatch police or ambulance.

In a life-threatening emergency, call 112 or your local emergency number. Misusing SOS to lure helpers is prohibited — see helper safety FAQ. You only need to accept this once.
